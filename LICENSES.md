The release includes benchmark identifiers, hashes, and numerical observations derived from [PRMBench Preview](https://huggingface.co/datasets/hitsmy/PRMBench_Preview/blob/5cc7683d0ae5797f84d7aeac0607966f277c39e1/README.md) and [ProcessBench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Qwen/ProcessBench/blob/3bdcd5371ed567559a78f559c01c13a6deee7604/README.md). Obtain question and solution text separately from the pinned datasets. Both dataset cards list [Apache 2.0](licenses/Apache-2.0.txt).

PRMBench draws on PRM800K and MATH, and the ProcessBench analysis uses its GSM8K subset. The respective upstream notices are included for reference. [PRM800K](licenses/PRM800K-MIT.txt) · [MATH](licenses/MATH-MIT.txt) · [GSM8K](licenses/GSM8K-MIT.txt)
